About OYLA

Made for the
years in between.

OYLA is a small children's line from Casablanca. We make a handful of pieces a year and make them properly — clothes that get passed to a younger cousin still looking like something you'd choose.

We started because most kids' clothing is built to be outgrown and thrown away. Ours is cut with room to grow, sewn from organic cotton, and finished to survive the wash, the playground, and the sibling after.

Everything is designed in-house and made in small runs in Portugal. We restock what works instead of chasing a new collection every season — fewer, better things, in a palette that doesn't shout.

Considered

Eight pieces, not eighty. Each one earns its place before it makes the line.

Durable

Reinforced where children actually wear through, and wash-tested sixty times.

Fairly made

Small runs, named workshops, and materials we can trace back to the field.
